Model zoo

Each model loads already trained, with notes on what to look for. The training history is kept, so you can scrub back and watch it learn.

How to read the network

Columns are layers, squares are neurons and curves are weighted connections. Signals flow left to right; error signals flow right to left during training.

Colour

Blue is positive and orange is negative everywhere: weights, activations, signals and the two classes of a binary problem. Violet measures gradients, the pressure to change.

The probe

One input is always being traced through the network. Hover over the Output map to move it, click to pin it, or click a data point to use that example. The Neuron tab shows its arithmetic.

Maps

With Maps on, each neuron shows what it outputs for every possible input, so you can watch simple shapes in the first layer combine into complex ones deeper in.
